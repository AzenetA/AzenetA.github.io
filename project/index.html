Welcome to Your Life in Knots!


This page was designed to learn how to crochet. As a beginner I thought I was never going to learn how to crochet. I also felt that rather than moving foward and completing my projects I would always end up with knots in my yarn. This website was made for a little support and getting better at a new hobby.

Some Projects that you could do if you were to learn crochet

  1. Scarves & Hats
  2. Blankets
  3. Clothing
  4. Hair Accessories
  5. ETC.

You may ask yourself, "what materials will I need to learn how to crochet?" Well all you'll need are crochet hooks and yarn. Below I will put some affordable options.

Affordable Yarn Brands


  • Lion Brand Offers a huge range from budget acrylics (Basic Stitch) to premium blends (Touch of Linen, Feels Like Butta) and cottons (24/7 Cotton).
  • CaronKnown for large, economical skeins like Caron One Pound, great for big projects.
  • Red Heart A staple for consistency and value, especially their acrylics.
  • These are just a few of the many affordable brands, you too can look for what works for you and is available in your area.
